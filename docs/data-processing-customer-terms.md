# NPS Me Data Processing and Customer Data Terms

Last updated: 13 May 2026

This document explains how NPS Me handles customer feedback data uploaded or imported into a private NPS Me workspace.

It is intended as practical customer-facing wording for early customers and pilots. It should be reviewed before being used as a formal legal Data Processing Agreement.

## 1. Roles and responsibilities

Where a customer uploads or imports personal data about their own customers, users, clients or respondents into NPS Me, the customer is normally the data controller.

NPS Me normally acts as a data processor for that uploaded workspace data, processing it only to provide the requested workspace, analysis, reporting and close-the-loop services.

The customer is responsible for ensuring that they have a valid lawful basis, appropriate notices, permissions and internal authority to provide the data to NPS Me for processing.

## 2. Types of data processed

Workspace data may include:

- Customer names
- Customer email addresses
- Company or account names
- NPS scores
- Survey answers
- Customer comments
- Submitted dates
- Journey stages or customer segments
- Selected survey options
- Close-the-loop notes and follow-up actions
- Imported source fields from CSV, JSON or connected systems

Customers should avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive or special category data.

Where names and email addresses are not required for follow-up, customers should consider using anonymised IDs instead.

## 3. Processing purposes

NPS Me processes workspace data to provide the requested services, including:

- Creating and managing private customer feedback workspaces
- Importing and saving feedback datasets
- Displaying NPS performance and response views
- Helping users review customer comments and feedback themes
- Generating AI-assisted summaries, risks and recommended actions
- Managing close-the-loop actions
- Supporting workspace access, security and audit logs
- Providing setup, support, troubleshooting and service improvement

NPS Me does not sell workspace data or use it for advertising.

## 4. AI-assisted analysis

Some NPS Me workspace features may use AI services to generate summaries, themes, risks, recommendations or suggested follow-up actions.

NPS Me aims to minimise the data sent for AI-assisted analysis. Where possible, AI analysis uses scores, buckets, comments, dates, selected options and relevant survey fields rather than unnecessary direct identifiers.

AI-generated outputs are assistive only. Customers should review AI outputs before using them for important decisions, customer communications or operational action.

## 5. Subprocessors and infrastructure

NPS Me may use trusted third-party providers to deliver the service. These may include:

- Heroku for application hosting
- Cloudflare for DNS, CDN, security and caching
- Supabase for database and workspace data storage
- OpenAI for AI-assisted analysis where AI features are used
- Formspree for contact form submissions where applicable

NPS Me aims to use appropriate safeguards and access controls when working with subprocessors.

## 6. Security measures

NPS Me uses reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect workspace data, including:

- HTTPS
- Private workspace login
- Individual user accounts
- Password hashing
- Workspace-level data separation
- Role-based restrictions for destructive actions
- Restricted backend access
- Usage and audit logging for key workspace events

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Customers should only upload data that they are authorised to process and should avoid unnecessary sensitive data.

## 7. Access control

Workspace access is restricted to authorised users.

Current workspace roles may include:

- Owner: main customer account holder with full workspace access
- Admin: trusted manager with workspace management rights
- Member: standard user who can import, view and analyse data but cannot delete datasets

NPS Me may assist with creating, updating or removing workspace users during early customer onboarding.

## 8. Data retention and deletion

Workspace datasets are retained while the workspace is active or while needed to provide the requested service, unless deletion is requested earlier by an authorised workspace owner or required by law.

Authorised workspace users may delete saved datasets where the product allows this.

When a dataset is deleted, related saved rows and close-the-loop actions may also be deleted.

NPS Me may retain limited logs or audit records where needed for security, troubleshooting, legal compliance or service records.

## 9. Customer requests and data subject rights

If NPS Me receives a request relating to personal data uploaded by a customer, NPS Me may refer the request to the customer where the customer is the data controller.

NPS Me will provide reasonable assistance where required to help the customer respond to valid access, correction, deletion or restriction requests.

## 10. Customer obligations

Customers agree to:

- Only upload data they are authorised to process
- Avoid unnecessary sensitive or special category data
- Ensure their own privacy notices and lawful basis are appropriate
- Use anonymised IDs where names/emails are not needed
- Keep workspace login details secure
- Remove users who should no longer have access
- Review AI outputs before acting on them
- Notify NPS Me promptly if they believe workspace data has been uploaded incorrectly or should be removed

## 11. Service limitations

NPS Me is intended to support customer feedback analysis, insight generation and close-the-loop workflows.

It is not intended to replace the customer’s CRM, support platform, legal record system or full data governance process.

The customer remains responsible for final decisions, customer communications and compliance with their own legal obligations.
