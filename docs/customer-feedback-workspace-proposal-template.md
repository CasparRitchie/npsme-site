# NPS Me Customer Feedback Workspace — Proposal Template

## 1. Customer situation

**Customer:** [Customer company name]
**Main contact:** [Name / role]
**Date:** [Date]

[Customer company] currently collects customer feedback through [Intercom / CSV exports / survey tools / forms / spreadsheets / other]. The team has useful NPS scores, comments and customer signals, but needs a clearer way to review feedback, identify themes, prioritise follow-up and turn customer comments into action.

The current challenge is:

- Feedback is stored across [tools / exports / spreadsheets].
- Comments are not reviewed consistently.
- NPS results are difficult to connect to operational follow-up.
- Detractor follow-up and close-the-loop actions are not always visible.
- The team wants something practical and lightweight rather than a heavy enterprise CX platform.

## 2. Proposed solution

NPS Me will set up a private customer feedback workspace for [Customer company].

The workspace will allow the team to:

- Import customer feedback datasets.
- Review NPS performance and distribution.
- Inspect individual customer responses and comments.
- Generate AI-assisted insight summaries.
- Identify themes, risks and recommended actions.
- Track close-the-loop follow-up actions.
- Give authorised users individual login access.

The aim is to create a working feedback operating layer that helps the team move from raw feedback to practical action.

## 3. What NPS Me will deliver

### Workspace setup

NPS Me will:

- Create a private workspace for [Customer company].
- Create initial authorised user accounts.
- Configure owner/admin/member access roles.
- Provide a sample CSV template and import guidance.
- Support the first data import.
- Confirm that workspace data is separated from other customer workspaces.

### Data import and analysis

NPS Me will help import the first customer feedback dataset and validate that key fields are detected correctly, including where available:

- Customer name or identifier
- Customer email, if required for follow-up
- NPS score
- Date submitted
- Customer comment
- Company or account
- Journey stage
- Selected options or extra survey answers

### Workspace views

The workspace will provide:

- Saved dataset list
- NPS performance view
- Response explorer
- AI-assisted insight summary
- Close-the-loop action queue
- Account/password management

### Close-the-loop support

NPS Me will help the customer identify how feedback should be followed up, including:

- Which detractors or risks should be prioritised
- Who should own follow-up
- What actions should be recorded
- How recurring themes should be reviewed by leadership or operations

## 4. What the customer needs to provide

[Customer company] should provide:

- A sample feedback export or CSV file.
- Confirmation that they are authorised to provide the data for processing.
- The names and emails of initial workspace users.
- A nominated workspace owner.
- Any relevant context about customer segments, journey stages, survey questions or support processes.

The customer should avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive data. Where names and email addresses are not required, anonymised IDs may be sufficient.

## 5. Data protection and privacy

NPS Me will provide the workspace as a private, access-controlled environment.

The customer remains responsible for ensuring they have a valid lawful basis and appropriate notices/permissions for any customer data they upload or provide.

NPS Me will use the uploaded data to provide the requested workspace, analysis and support services. Workspace data will not be sold or used for advertising.

AI-assisted insight features may process feedback comments, scores and related survey fields to generate summaries, themes, risks and recommended actions. AI outputs should be reviewed by a human before being used for important decisions or customer communications.

Relevant subprocessors may include hosting, infrastructure, database and AI providers such as Heroku, Cloudflare, Supabase and OpenAI.

## 8. Assumptions

This proposal assumes:

- The customer can provide feedback data in a usable export format.
- The first version will use CSV/JSON-style imports unless an integration is agreed separately.
- NPS Me is not replacing the customer’s CRM, support platform or survey tool.
- The workspace is intended to support analysis, follow-up and decision-making, not to act as the customer’s sole system of record.
- AI outputs are assistive and should be reviewed by a human.

- The customer understands how to repeat the import/review process.
